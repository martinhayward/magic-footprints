Frequently Asked Questions

What will I receive in each product?

Everything you'll need to start making memories is right inside the package. Each kit includes a footprint stencil, a package of "magic" glitter, a flour applicator, a Santa "Thank You" note, as well as easy to read instructions for applications.

How soon can I expect my order to ship?

All orders are shipped via USPS within the continental United States. You can anticipate your parcel to arrive within 3 to 4 days after you've placed your order.

How can I draw attention to the footprints?

The glitter works wonders in drawing children's eyes to the magic footprints. Also placing the personalized card, and a glass of milk and plate of cookies (bite out of the cookies, of course!) adds to your little ones Christmas morning memories!

How large are the stencils?

Each stencil is 12" tall, 6" Wide.

Are the stencils reusable?

Yes. They are made of high quality plastic and/or mylar and with proper care should last through your children's "I believe in Santa Claus" years!

What other materials can be used to make the prints?

You may use Flour, Carpet Deodorizer, Corn Starch, Baking Soda, or Baby Powder to make excellent footprints.

Can the prints be placed on carpets?

Yes, however the darker the surface, like bricks on a fireplace, driveways, front doorsteps, or wood surfaces, the more defined the prints will look.

Be mindful ALL powder type materials can be challenging on vacuuming up depending on the quality of your vacuum cleaner, so we DO NOT recommend placing on carpet.

Vacuuming tips: For the glitter, sprinkle it with a bit of baby powder or baking soda to help minimize the static of the glitter making vacuuming easier.
